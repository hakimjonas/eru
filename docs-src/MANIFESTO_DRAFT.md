### The Eru Manifesto

**Our Purpose:** We craft `Eru` with the patience of ages, seeking to create software that serves the art of programming itself. Born from devotion to correctness and joy in creation, `Eru` offers what we have learned through countless iterations: that true power emerges not from complexity, but from the harmony of simple, perfect things.

In the tradition of those who came before, we tend this craft with reverence - knowing that each line of code is both gift and responsibility.

---

### Pillar I: Foundational Correctness
*(The Bedrock of All That Follows)*

We serve correctness as our highest calling, knowing that from this foundation all good things flow. Like ancient craftsmen who would not compromise the integrity of their work, we pursue a level of correctness so complete it becomes invisible - allowing those who follow to build with absolute trust.

-   **Pure Program Representation:** `Eru[E, A]` stands as an immutable testament to what shall be - a total description that suspends the world's chaos within ordered thought.
-   **Type-Directed Wisdom:** We entrust our designs to the discipline of types, using Scala 3's gifts - opaque types, GADTs, and compositional structures - to encode truth so that falsehood cannot even be expressed.
-   **Law-Abiding Nature:** Like the music that underlies all creation, `Eru` follows laws that cannot be broken - verified through property-based tests that prove our faithfulness to functional principles.

---

### Pillar II: Radical Ergonomics  
*(Joy in the Making)*

True power should feel like breathing - natural, effortless, life-giving. We strive to offer tools that bring joy to their use, believing that the most beautiful code emerges when complexity bows to clarity.

-   **Intuitive Expression:** Common patterns - retries, timeouts, resource management - flow as naturally as thought itself, discoverable and direct upon the `Eru` type.
-   **The Path of Clarity:** We choose simplicity over cleverness, directness over ceremony, knowing that the most elegant solutions are often the most straightforward.

---

### Pillar III: Guided Correctness
*(The Easy Path, The Right Path)*

We believe good architecture should whisper the correct choice in the developer's ear. Like a path through an ancient forest, the way forward should feel both natural and safe.

-   **Resource Harmony:** Through `Eru.Resource` and `bracket`/`ensure` patterns, we encode the natural rhythm of acquire-use-release, making resource safety feel inevitable rather than effortful.
-   **Concurrency as Craft:** High-level primitives abstract the dangerous complexities of concurrent execution, allowing developers to compose parallel solutions with confidence.
-   **Respectful Boundaries:** When we must bridge to blocking operations, `Eru.blocking(...)` creates clear sanctuary - protecting the application's responsiveness by design.

---

### Pillar IV: Transparent Runtime
*(Light in Dark Places)*

A program's execution should not be shrouded in mystery. We build `Eru` to be knowable - its workings visible to those who seek to understand.

-   **Structured Truth:** Errors carry meaning in their very structure - rich, typed information rather than opaque mysteries.
-   **Gentle Observation:** Tracing and timing flow naturally through the system, available without burden, respecting the performance of the code they observe.
-   **Unified Vision:** The `EruObserver` offers one clear window into program execution, compatible with the tools and systems developers already trust.

---

**The Promise**

These four pillars stand as our covenant - with those who use `Eru`, with the craft we serve, and with ourselves. They represent not what we shall conquer, but what we shall tend and protect.

`Eru` stands ready to serve those who, like us, believe that software can be both powerful and beautiful, that correctness and joy need not be enemies, that the best tools disappear into the work they enable.

*In this spirit, the validation library `Valar` shall be reborn upon `Eru`'s foundation, unifying effect, error, and validation into one harmonious whole.*
